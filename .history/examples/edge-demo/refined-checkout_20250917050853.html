🧪 How to Test the Refined Checkout

  1. Look for the JENNi pill in the bottom-right corner
  2. Click the pill to open the store selection panel
  3. Click on any store to select it (notice the visual feedback)
  4. Click "Deliver with JENNi" to open the refined checkout modal
  5. Observe the two-column layout with product info on the left
  6. Try different payment methods (Amex, PayPal, Credit Card)
  7. Test form validation by clearing the email field
  8. Complete the order to see the tracking confirmation
  9. Test on mobile by resizing your browser window
