JENNi Edge - Corner Selection

Test the new corner selection functionality where retailer names are clickable links and store selection happens via a corner checkbox.

How to Test:

  • Click the JENNi pill that appears in the bottom-right corner
  • In the panel, notice each store has a checkbox in the top-right corner
  • Click the checkbox to select a store (not the entire row)
  • Click the retailer name to visit their website or search for the product
  • Special: Click "Best Buy" to see the specific Sony ULT Field 3 product page
  • Selected stores will show a blue checkmark in the corner
  • Try the "Deliver with JENNi" button to see the checkout flow
